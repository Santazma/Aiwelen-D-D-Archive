Traits
Frozen Burning Storm Giant. Koralith has two phases. In his first phase, he deals cold damage. In his second phase, he starts to deal fire damage. If Koralith drops to 100 hit points, he shifts to his second phase.
Aura of Pressure. All flying creatures within 60 ft. roll a DC 16 Strength save at the start of each round. Those that fail fall to the ground.
Freedom of Movement. Koralith ignores difficult terrain, and magical effects can't reduce his speed or cause him to be restrained.
Regeneration. Koralith regains 10 hit points at the start of his turn. This regeneration stops for 1 turn if he is hit by fire damage or he is reduced to 0 hit points.
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If Koralith fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
Actions
Multiattack. In his first phase, Koralith first attacks with Valkørr's Oath Hammer, then he can use Lava/Frost Smash or Igneous/Freezing Eruption. In his second phase, Koralith can use any of his attacks in any order he desires.
Valkørr's Oath Hammer. Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 2d10+7 bludgeoning damage.
Lava/Frost Smash. 20 ft. wide and 40 ft. long, delayed AoE attack. Explodes after 1 turn. DC 19 Dexterity save: 3d8 fire or cold damage plus 3d6 bludgeoning damage.
Igneous/Freezing Eruption (Recharge 5–6). 20 ft. radius, delayed AoE. Explodes after 1 turn. DC 19 Dexterity save: 5d10 fire or cold damage.
Thunder Rend (Second Phase). 60 ft. long, 10 ft. wide line. Hits after 1 turn. DC 19 Constitution save: 6d8 thunder damage. If the creature fails the saving throw, it becomes stunned.
Magma Pillars (1/Rest) (Second Phase). Koralith summons four pillars of eternal fire. Each pillar has 40 hit points and an Armor Class of 10. The pillars share the same initiative, which is 10. After 3 rounds, these pillars summon a Fire Elemental. To halt the summoning, all pillars must be destroyed. If even one pillar remains standing, the count continues, repeating this process for a maximum of three cycles. Also, while even one pillar remains standing, Koralith takes half damage from all incoming damage.
Lair Actions
On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Koralith may take a lair action to trigger one of the following effects. The same effect can't be used two rounds in a row.
Eruption Mark. Two areas on the lair with a radius of 20 ft. start bubbling with magma. After 1 lap, these two fields explode. DC 14 Dexterity save: 2d12 fire damage.
Movement Restriction. One designated creature must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw, or its speed is halved for 1 turn.
