Traits
Necrotic Adept. Ophelia's attacks deal necrotic damage and she ignores necrotic resistance.
Shadow Teleportation. Ophelia can teleport onto another creature's shadow as a bonus action.
Abyssal Isolation Restoration. When Ophelia uses her Abyssal Isolation feature, she regenerates 30 HP.
Shadow Cloak. When Ophelia uses her Abyssal Isolation feature, she is considered Half Covered and her AC gets +2.
Shapechanger. Ophelia can use her action to polymorph into a Medium humanoid she has consumed, or back into her true form (a shadowy, many-eyed aberration). While transformed, her stats remain the same, but she mimics the appearance, mannerisms, and voice perfectly.
Abyssal Hunger. Whenever Ophelia reduces a creature to 0 hit points, she can consume their essence as a bonus action. If she does, she directly kills her target, regains 30 hit points, and can mimic one of their voices and surface memories.
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If Ophelia fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
Actions
Multiattack. Ophelia makes 2 attacks with her Rending Shadows or uses Shadow Grasp once among her attacks.
Multiattack (In Abyssal Isolation). In Abyssal Isolation, Ophelia makes 3 attacks with her Dread Lines, Abyssal Gaze, and Delayed Death Line, each consecutively.
Rending Shadows. Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 22 (3d6+6) necrotic damage.
Shadow Grasp. Shadowy hands erupt in a 40-foot, 45° cone originating from Ophelia. Each creature in the cone must make a DC 17 Strength save or be Restrained until the end of their next turn. Restrained creatures take 17 (5d6) necrotic damage.
Dread Lines. 8 lines of decaying shadows appear, each 90 feet long, 10 feet wide, and 60 feet high, standing side by side. These Dread Lines have a delay and take effect at the start of Ophelia's next turn. When that turn begins, everyone caught in the lines must make a DC 20 Constitution saving throw, taking 8d6 necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a success.
Abyssal Gaze. Lines of harrowing gazes appear, each 40 feet long and 10 feet wide, originating from abyssal eyes. These Abyssal Gaze Lines have a delay and take effect at the start of Ophelia's next turn. When that turn begins, everyone caught in the lines must make a DC 20 Constitution saving throw, taking 12d4 psychic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a success.
Delayed Death Line. One long death line appears, 120 feet long, 10 feet wide, and 20 feet high, originating from Ophelia. This Death Line has a delay and takes effect at the start of Ophelia's next turn. When that turn begins, everyone caught in the line must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10d10 necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a success.
Signature Mechanic: Abyssal Isolation (Recharge 6). At the start of her turn, Ophelia tries to mark one creature she can see within 120 feet. The target must make a DC 17 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, the target and Ophelia are pulled into an isolated demiplane, and a ball of black slime forms where the target was standing. This massive ball of slime has AC 10 and 150 HP; destroying it ends the Abyssal Isolation.
Signature Mechanic: Abyssal Isolation Arena Rules. After being pulled into the demiplane, the target's hit points become equal to the total hit points of all living creatures currently fighting Ophelia, and its maximum hit points become equal to the highest maximum HP among those players. In this arena, the fight against Ophelia resets and begins again: the target takes a number of turns equal to the total number of players in the fight, rerolling that many initiative rolls, while Ophelia rerolls the same and takes only one turn. The duel lasts 3 rounds. Whoever deals more damage over those 3 rounds wins. If Ophelia wins, the total damage she dealt is divided among all the players, each taking that amount as psychic damage. If the target wins, Ophelia takes no damage but returns to the battlefield Paralyzed until the start of her next turn.
Legendary Actions
Ophelia can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Ophelia regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.
Dark Chains (1 Action). A chosen creature must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or become tethered; until the start of Ophelia's next turn, it takes 10 necrotic damage at the start of its turns and cannot move more than 15 ft. from the tether point.
Abyssal Stake Shot (1 Action). Ophelia makes a ranged attack against one creature. Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 60 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d8+6) necrotic damage. If the attack hits, the target makes a DC 17 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, Ophelia learns one surface memory.
Perpetual Shell (1 Action). Ophelia shields herself with a shadow shell, gaining resistance against the next incoming attack's damage. This resistance is valid only for that one attack and disappears once she takes damage from it.
Lair Actions
On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Ophelia may take a lair action to trigger one of the following effects. The same effect can't be used two rounds in a row.
Crumbling Reality. Two circles with a radius of 30 feet appear in the lair. These abyssal shadow circles have a delay and take effect at the start of the lair's next turn. When that turn begins, everyone caught in the circles must make a DC 17 Charisma saving throw, taking 2d10 necrotic damage on a failed save (half as much on a success), and creatures who fail become stunned until the start of their next turn.
Summon Shadow. 1d6 Shadows appear and act immediately after Ophelia.
