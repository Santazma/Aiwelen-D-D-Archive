Traits
Charismatic Resilience. Discount has advantage on Charisma saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
Enchanted Overcoat (1/Short Rest). As a free action, Discount can activate his magical overcoat. For 1 minute, he gains a flying speed of 60 feet. He can use this trait once per short rest.
Innate Sorcery (2/Long Rest). As a bonus action, Discount unleashes his innate magical power for 1 minute. During this time, his spell save DC increases by 1 (to DC 19), and he has advantage on attack rolls for sorcerer spells he casts.
Font of Magic & Metamagic. Discount has 14 Sorcery Points that he can expend to modify his spells. He can use Transmuted Spell and Quickened Spell options.
Divine Intervention (1/7 Days). Discount can call on a divine deity for assistance. He describes the assistance he seeks and uses his action to request it. The deity intervenes in a manner appropriate to Discount's alignment and immediate need. Once used, Discount cannot use this trait again for 7 days.
Spellcasting. Discount is a high-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 18, +10 to hit with spell attacks). He has the following spells prepared:
Cantrips (at will): Fire Bolt, Light, Sacred Flame, Guidance
1st level (4 slots): Absorb Elements, Shield, Chromatic Orb, Burning Hands
2nd level (3 slots): Enhance Ability, Scorching Ray, Misty Step
3rd level (3 slots): Beacon of Hope, Fireball, Counterspell
4th level (3 slots): Wall of Fire, Divination, Locate Creature
5th level (2 slots): Teleportation Circle, Flame Strike
6th level (1 slot): Sunbeam
7th level (1 slot): Delayed Blast Fireball
8th level (1 slot): Sunburst
Actions
Cast a Spell. Discount can casts one of his Spells using an Action or a Cantrip. Additionally he can cast a spell using Quickened Spell as a bonus action.
Misty Step (Bonus Action). Discount instantly teleportation up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see.
Reactions
Shield. When Discount is hit by an attack or targeted by Magic Missile, he gains a +5 bonus to AC (including against the triggering attack) and takes no damage from Magic Missile until the start of his next turn.
Absorb Elements. When Discount takes acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage, he gains resistance to that damage type until the start of his next turn, including against the triggering effect.
Counterspell. When a creature Discount can see within 60 feet casts a spell, Discount attempts to interrupt it. If the spell is 3rd level or lower, it fails. If higher, Discount makes a Charisma check (DC 10 + spell's level); on a success, the spell fails.
