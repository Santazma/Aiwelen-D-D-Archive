Shadow Blend. In dim light or darkness, Mordrek can take the Hide action as a bonus action.
Magic Resistance. Mordrek has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
Actions
Multiattack. Mordrek makes two Claw attacks and one Bite attack.
Claw. Melee Attack Roll: +6, reach 5 ft. Hit: 1d8+3 slashing damage.
Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +6, reach 5 ft. Hit: 2d6+3 piercing damage plus 1d6 poison damage.
Frightful Gaze (Recharge 5–6). Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 15, one creature Mordrek can see within 30 feet. Failure: The target has the Frightened condition for 1 minute. The target can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
Legendary Actions
Mordrek can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Mordrek regains all expended legendary actions at the start of its turn.
Move. Mordrek moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
Claw Attack. Mordrek makes one Claw attack.
Shadow Step (Costs 2 Actions). Mordrek teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
